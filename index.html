Case Study — Equivital | Journal, 2025
Conformal Deep Learning for Core Body Temperature
Role: Conceived study, designed/implemented conformal deep learning framework, developed SCOPE algorithm, performed all ML experiments/analyses, wrote manuscript.

Case Study — voestalpine
Advanced Materials & Processes at voestalpine
Role: Key ML lead from Intellegens supporting voestalpine across 4 use cases—new AM alloy design (identified critical chromium concentration + martensitic phase importance), process parameter optimization, QA KPI generation from sensor/monitoring data, and operational model deployment. Ensured teams understood the tools, why they work, and how to translate ML into their domain.
Impact: Achieved almost 40% cost savings over 3 years in process-parameter optimization/testing/manufacturing; reduced iterations from multiple tests to one round of testing; equipped broader teams with validated models.
Case Study — Yili
Developing Food Formulations at Yili
Role: Project lead—data formatting, hierarchical time-series ML modelling (predicting 9-month shelf life), insight extraction via XAI to identify key ingredient drivers among 9 stabilizers and 15 emulsifiers.
Impact: Enabled ingredient down-selection from 24 ingredient options; predicted later-month shelf-life behavior from early tests; modeled 15,000 data points spanning 2–3 years with good R² performance.
Case Study — OCAS (ArcelorMittal)
Improving Steel Performance with OCAS
Role: Project lead—built multi-modal PSP models integrating chemistry, treatments (23 unique), processing variables (9), and microstructure images encoded via deep learning + PCA; led process parameter optimization using XAI.
Impact: Achieved R² > 0.8 property prediction across 4 material chemistries; identified optimal image magnification; reduced imaging data by up to 30% without accuracy loss in some circumstances.
Case Study — The AMRC
Composite Manufacturing with The AMRC
Role: Project lead—built sparse deep learning model with preprocessing pipeline (grouping + aggregation) to predict tooling failure mechanisms across 55 drill/composite pairs with 23 machining responses; used XAI to streamline experiments.
Impact: Achieved R² = 0.73 despite 82% missing targets; enabled 80% fewer experiments (200 vs typical 1,000 holes) → 80%+ reduction in direct testing costs; predicted tool life from start-of-life performance.
Case Study — FUCHS
Lubricant Formulation Development at FUCHS
Role: Secured pilot engagement, trained their key champion in ML/adaptive DOE workflows, provided ongoing technical support throughout—champion executed work on their side using 24 historic formulations across 3 DOE cycles generating 30 candidates.
Impact: Achieved 50% performance improvement target; chemist selected 7 of 30 ML-generated candidates to test → 2 met target and were shared with customer; delivered goals in significantly reduced timescales vs traditional approaches.
Case Study — BAT, Zizo, B-Secur | Journal, 2024
Nicotine Pharmacokinetic Modelling for E-Cigarettes
Role: Led all machine learning modelling end-to-end—data preparation, model development, validation, analysis, and results translation.
Impact: Published in BioData Mining. Validated ML approach for pharmacokinetic predictions across multiple industry partners (BAT, Zizo, B-Secur).
